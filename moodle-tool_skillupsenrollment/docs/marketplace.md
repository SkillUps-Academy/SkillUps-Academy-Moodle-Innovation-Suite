Simulate and schedule bulk enrollments, additive role assignments, group memberships, suspensions and reactivations through native Moodle APIs. Each row gets its own verification, execution status and recoverable error handling. Reports are available per course, user or batch. Community operates locally with optional Course Factory integration.
