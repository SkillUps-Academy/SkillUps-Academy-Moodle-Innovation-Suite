# Moodle Marketplace submission draft

- **Name** — SkillUps Gamification Pro
- **Component** — `local_skillupsgamification`
- **Release** — `0.9.0-beta.1`
- **License** — GPL-3.0-or-later

**Short description** — Reward verified Moodle completion with points, levels, goals and privacy-aware course rankings.

**Full description** — Configure idempotent awards, missions, badges, individual and team challenges, time-limited events and limited-inventory rewards. Choose from Moodle groupings for teams. Learners review their progress and opt into course rankings under an alias. Administrators configure rules per course and record reward delivery. An optional local provider contract supports Learning Path. Community operates without external services.

The direct-install package includes English and Spanish strings and bundles no third-party libraries. No Composer installation is required. SkillUps Academy maintains the suite independently of Moodle.
