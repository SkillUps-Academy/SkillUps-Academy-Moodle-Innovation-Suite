## Short English description

Review Moodle assignment evidence and rubric-based AI suggestions with teachers in control of grading.

## Full English description

SkillUps AI Grading Assistant brings assignment submissions and native rubrics into a private teacher review workspace. Teachers can prepare a local evidence worksheet or send an approved evidence excerpt to a configured model. The assistant displays criterion-level suggestions, verified excerpts, possible omissions, uncertainty and editable feedback. It records human decisions and can compare suggestions with Moodle's gradebook in read-only mode. Grades and feedback are published from Moodle's native grader. Community operates independently of SkillUps Cloud.
