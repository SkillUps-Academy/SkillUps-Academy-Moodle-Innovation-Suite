## Short English description

Review explainable academic risk signals and coordinate tutor support in Moodle.

## Full English description

SkillUps Student Success Predictor monitors academic signals for opt-in courses, creates support cases, and gives tutors an authorized workspace for intervention tracking. Deterministic rules and optional fixed-model inference have separate explanations and evaluation. Prospective outcome samples include learners with and without alerts, with privacy-protected comparisons across courses and programs. Academic decisions remain with the responsible staff.
