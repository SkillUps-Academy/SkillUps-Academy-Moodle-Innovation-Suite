# Moodle Marketplace submission materials

Component `mod_skillupsattendance` · Activity module · SkillUps Academy · GNU GPL v3 or later.

## Short English description

Manage, record and review attendance for classroom, online and hybrid Moodle sessions with scoped reporting and optional Gradebook publication.

## Full English description

SkillUps Attendance Pro schedules individual and recurring sessions, provides teacher marking and student check-in, supports temporary QR and access codes, and records entry and exit. Learners can submit private justification evidence. Authorized staff can review requests, assess attendance and risk at activity or course level, export results, and explicitly publish a Gradebook snapshot.

The activity uses Moodle groups, calendar, files, tasks, messaging, Gradebook, backup and the Privacy API. Community includes operational reports and CSV and Excel exports through the site's data formats. Optional signed entitlements enable an audit-evidence CSV. The distribution includes English and Spanish.
