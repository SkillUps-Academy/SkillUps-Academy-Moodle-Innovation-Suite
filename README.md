# SkillUps Academy Moodle Innovation Suite

- [SkillUps AI Copilot](moodle-local_skillupscopilot/README.md) — Moodle assistant for students, teachers and administrators.
- [SkillUps Flow Automation](moodle-local_skillupsflow/README.md) — Visual workflows with Moodle triggers, native actions and human approvals.
- [SkillUps Question Studio](moodle-local_skillupsquestionstudio/README.md) — Author and review course questions and publish approved assessments to the Moodle question bank.
- [SkillUps AI Grading Assistant](moodle-local_skillupsaigrading/README.md) — Review assignment evidence and rubric-based suggestions before grading in Moodle.
- [SkillUps Insight Analytics](moodle-report_skillupsinsight/README.md) — Compare academic periods, publish scoped analytics snapshots, and track institutional targets.
- [SkillUps Student Success Predictor](moodle-local_skillupssuccess/README.md) — Review explainable risk signals, coordinate tutor support, and measure prospective outcomes.
- [SkillUps System Monitor](moodle-report_skillupsmonitor/README.md) — Monitor Moodle observations, cron, task queues and incidents.
- [SkillUps Course Quality Inspector](moodle-report_skillupsquality/README.md) — Audit course configuration and track corrective plans.
- [SkillUps Course Factory](moodle-tool_skillupscoursefactory/README.md) — Preview, schedule and provision individual or batch course operations.
- [SkillUps Smart Enrollment Manager](moodle-tool_skillupsenrollment/README.md) — Simulate and process enrollment, role, group and status changes in batches.
- [SkillUps Gradebook Guardian](moodle-local_skillupsgradebookguardian/README.md) — Diagnose gradebook configuration and approve reversible visibility repairs.
- [SkillUps Helpdesk](moodle-local_skillupshelpdesk/README.md) — Operate a scoped support desk with secure attachments, business-time SLAs and reports.
- [SkillUps Gamification Pro](moodle-local_skillupsgamification/README.md) — Award verified learning progress, coordinate course challenges and protect reward inventory.
- [SkillUps Learning Path Designer](moodle-local_skillupspathways/README.md) — Design versioned cross-course pathways and follow individual and group progress.
- [SkillUps Certify & Verify](moodle-local_skillupscertify/README.md) — Design and issue archived PDF certificates, verify QR codes and manage revocation.
