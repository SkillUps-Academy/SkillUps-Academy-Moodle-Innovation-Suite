# Moodle Marketplace submission draft

- **Plugin name** — SkillUps Helpdesk
- **Component** — `local_skillupshelpdesk`
- **Release** — `0.9.0-beta.1`, beta
- **License** — GPL-3.0-or-later

**Short description** — Run a Moodle-native support desk with private tickets, business-hour service goals, technician assignment and scoped reports.

**Full description** — SkillUps Helpdesk offers a requester portal and staff queue, configurable statuses, severity and priority, protected attachments, internal notes, first response and resolution SLAs, escalation, generic Moodle notifications, a knowledge base, response templates and satisfaction surveys. Staff can use scoped reports by technician, campus, course and category. Community works independently. Signed entitlements optionally enable JSON export.

The package bundles no third-party libraries and requires no Composer installation. English is canonical and the direct-install ZIP carries Spanish localization. The SkillUps Academy suite is maintained independently of Moodle. Link the validation evidence and screenshots before Marketplace review.
