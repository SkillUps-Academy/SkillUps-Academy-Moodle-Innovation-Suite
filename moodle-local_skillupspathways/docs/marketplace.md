## Short description

Design versioned learning pathways across Moodle courses and activities, and track progress against prerequisites, grades and competencies.

## Full description

Build a visual pathway with required and optional steps, alternative branches and AND/OR prerequisites. Link activities or full courses across categories. Moodle enrollment and visibility determine what each participant can access. A pathway uses native completion, visible numeric grades and course competency proficiency to recommend the next steps. Learners keep their assigned version until an authorized manager confirms migration. Course staff can follow progress and filter their reports by groups.

El ZIP tiene un único directorio `skillupspathways` con `version.php`, el código del plugin y su catálogo inglés. La suite publica su código en un monorepositorio. Antes de presentar el paquete, confirma el tratamiento de `lang/es` y el registro en AMOS con el proveedor de Moodle Marketplace. La presentación y revisión oficial siguen pendientes.
